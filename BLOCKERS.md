# FUTUREGROW AI — External Blockers

A blocker is something this build cannot complete or verify without a credential, external approval, paid service, legal decision or infrastructure capability. Code that *can* be written safely is written and tested; the blocked part fails closed (deny / read-only / queue-for-human) and never reports fake success.
